# Ideas: things to make with one button, a screen and a buzzer

The chat picks from here when you don't have an idea yet, or mixes them with yours. Stars = how much there is to build.

## First programs (*)

* **My name in lights**: your name, big, with a frame that blinks when you press.
* **Press counter**: how many presses in 10 seconds? Show a bar that fills.
* **Mood face**: each press changes the face: happy, surprised, sleepy.
* **Doorbell**: a tune when the button is pressed.
* **Morse sender**: short press = dot, long press = dash; shows the letters.

## Games (** to ***)

* **Jump** (examples/jump_game): jump over blocks; make it a cat, a car, a dinosaur.
* **Flappy-style**: hold to fly up, let go to fall, gaps in pipes.
* **Reaction timer**: wait for "NOW!", press as fast as you can, see your milliseconds. Pressing early = foul!
* **Stop the bar**: a bar sweeps left and right; press when it is in the middle zone. Faster each round.
* **Simon with one button**: the kit beeps a rhythm; tap it back.
* **Catch**: things fall; tap to move left/right (tap = change direction).
* **Lane runner**: tap to switch between two lanes; avoid the cars.
* **Space shooter**: the ship moves by itself; press to fire.
* **Rhythm game**: notes scroll by; tap on the beat.

## Gadgets (** to ***)

* **Stopwatch and lap timer**: tap = start/lap, hold = reset.
* **Dice**: press to roll, with an animation and a sound.
* **Pomodoro timer**: 25 minutes of work, 5 of rest, with a progress ring.
* **Musical instrument**: hold for a note; the longer you hold, the higher.
* **Pet**: a little creature to feed (tap) and play with (hold); it gets hungry over time. Saves its state.

## Internet (***; needs WiFi, 2.4 GHz)

* **Clock**: the exact time from the internet, analog or digital.
* **Weather**: today's temperature for your city.
* **Countdown**: days until the holiday / the end of school.
* **Two kits talking** (ESP-NOW): press on one, the other beeps. Needs two kits.

## For teachers

Each game above teaches something: the jump teaches gravity and collision; the reaction timer, `millis()` and fairness;
Simon, arrays and memory; the pet, state machines and saving. See the `kit-mentor` skill's concept table.
